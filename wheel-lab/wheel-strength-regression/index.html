Lateral Strength Regressor

This is an experimental tool that estimates how different hub, rim, and build choices affect a wheel's lateral strength — the sideways force it can take before a spoke loses tension. It's built on 30 real hub designs across a range of rim and build combinations, but the rim stiffness numbers used here are this tool's own estimates, not measured data.
