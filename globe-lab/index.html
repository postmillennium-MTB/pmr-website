    What this prototype is testing

    • Equal area, provably. The rhombic triacontahedron is face-transitive — all 30 countries are congruent, each exactly 4π/30 sr. A soccer-ball tessellation only looks equal; its hexagons are ~1.7× its pentagons.
    • Adjacency carries meaning. Tools are grouped into five contiguous continents by category. A globe of 21 scattered countries is worse than a list; a globe where related tools border each other is better.
    • Vector, not bitmap. Every face is a real SVG path — clickable, focusable, screen-reader labelled, and retinted by CSS variables across all five themes with no per-theme assets.
    • Nine unclaimed faces are headroom to 30 tools. Past that the next exactly-equal-area step up is 60 faces.
