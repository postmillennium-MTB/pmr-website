Tap bare ground to walk, or use the arrow keys. Walk off the edge of the screen to go to the next one. Tap a verb, then tap something in the scene. Tap an item in your pack to use it on the scene, or tap a second item to combine the two. Choose Talk, then tap a character to start a conversation. With a mouse: right-click cycles verbs, keys 1 to 4 pick them (or pick replies while talking), M opens the map, C reads the compass, H or the HINT button says plainly what to do next, Esc opens the save menu. The game autosaves in this browser.

Prototype 0.6: 21 screens including a woods trail, an orchard, a marsh, an enchanted grotto and a cave system under Fox Hollow, 3 characters to talk to, one ending, autosave and save codes. 16 EGA colors, 320 by 200.
