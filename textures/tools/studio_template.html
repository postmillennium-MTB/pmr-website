Credits

    
Every pattern here comes from a US design patent for a carpet by Eugene A. Crowe of Boston, 1882-1884. The patents expired more than a century ago. Drawing scans are from the US Patent Office via Google Patents.

    
    
    
    Patterns marked Adapted are not the patent's own drawing: the drawing was redrawn with an AI image model (Google Gemini) and its edges repaired so it tiles. Only patterns marked Patent drawing are the original drawing.
