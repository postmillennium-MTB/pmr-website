About this tool

It makes things up. TINY knows about 3,500 facts (countries, elements, animals, sums, spelling, word forms) asked in many wordings. Outside those it answers confidently and wrongly. On 780 unseen test questions its author measured about 61% correct; on questions worded the way it was taught, about 90%. Treat the confidence cues as a hint, not a guarantee.

  • Size: 4 layers, 96 wide, 367,000 parameters, 8-bit weights with one scale per row, 380 KB. It is the DOS release's exact network file.
  • How it runs here: a JavaScript port of the original C inference code. Your questions never leave your device. The port was checked against the native C build, letter for letter, on 49 questions at creativity 0.
  • Creativity sets how willing it is to pick a slightly less likely next letter, so the same question can be worded differently each time.
  • Things to ask: capitals and borders, element symbols, “what is 7 times 8”, “spell elephant”, “plural of mouse”, animal babies and sounds, a few words in French, German, Spanish and Italian.
